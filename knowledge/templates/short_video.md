# Short-video template

## Hook
One clear opening line; avoid misleading claims.

## Script
- Context/problem
- 2–3 useful points
- Specific example
- Clear close / call to action

## Caption
Concise summary and relevant hashtags.

## Visual direction
Original shots, screen recordings, or assets with permission.

## Sources
URLs supporting factual claims.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Claims verified
- [ ] No copyrighted assets used without permission
- [ ] Brand voice checked
- [ ] Platform requirements checked
- [ ] Human approved
